# Content Ideas — Extraction Reference

Loaded by the Content Ideas vector for every call, regardless of call type.
This vector always runs. Extract content value from any call — sales, internal, or general.

## Purpose

Surface the 3–5 most quotable moments and 3–5 strongest content angles from the transcript.
Output feeds directly into Mitch's content backlog for LinkedIn and newsletter drafts.

## Quotable Moments

Extract 3–5 moments that meet ALL of these criteria:

- Verbatim quote from the transcript (no paraphrasing — the exact words)
- Stands alone without context (a reader who wasn't on the call gets it)
- Contains a specific insight, surprising stat, contrarian take, or emotionally resonant observation
- 1–3 sentences maximum

For each quotable moment, provide:

- **Quote:** [exact verbatim text]
- **Speaker:** [who said it — "Mitch" or prospect name/role]
- **Context:** [one sentence — what prompted this in the call]
- **Content potential:** [why this is usable — what makes it quotable]

Avoid:

- Generic agreement ("Yeah, exactly, that makes sense")
- Process descriptions without insight ("So then we'd set up the sequences")
- Transitional phrases without substance

## Content Angles

Extract 3–5 angles that could become LinkedIn posts, newsletter sections, or content atoms.

For each angle, provide:

- **Hook:** [first line of the post — concrete, specific, no filler]
- **Format suggestion:** [hot-take / process-reveal / client-success / quick-tip / live-dispatch]
- **Core idea:** [2–3 sentences on what the post argues or reveals]
- **Source moment:** [which part of the call this came from]

Format options:

- **hot-take:** Contrarian or counterintuitive claim backed by experience
- **process-reveal:** Behind-the-scenes of how something actually works
- **client-success:** Specific result with the mechanism that produced it (no named companies without permission)
- **quick-tip:** Single tactical insight with immediate applicability
- **live-dispatch:** Raw, in-the-moment observation — lowercase, casual, no structure

Avoid:

- Angles that require extensive context to land
- Angles that reveal confidential prospect information
- Generic outbound advice that anyone could write
